## मूल कविता (हिंदी/उर्दू)

ऊपर नौकरी की बारी थी
दरबान ने सीढ़ी दिखलाई

कपड़ों से मुझको तौला था
पिछला दर मुझको बतलाया

मैं आगे के दर से लौटा
सीढ़ी खाली पड़ी रही

ऊपर सबने नाम पुकारा
नीचे मेरी बारी गई

## English Translation

Upstairs, my turn for the job had come;
the doorman pointed out the stairs.

He had weighed me by my clothes
and directed me to the back entrance.

I turned away from the front door;
the staircase remained empty.

Upstairs, they called my name;
down below, my turn was lost.
