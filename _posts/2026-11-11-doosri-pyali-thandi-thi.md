## मूल कविता (हिंदी/उर्दू)

ख़त को चूल्हे में जलाया मैंने ही
धुआँ कमरे में बुलाया मैंने ही

तेरी चाबी अब मेरी मुट्ठी में थी
ये बंद दरवाज़ा बनाया मैंने ही

तूने बस आधा कहा उस रात मुझे
बाक़ी मतलब भी लगाया मैंने ही

तेरी हर भूल मैंने गिनाई सबको
अपना हिस्सा भी छुपाया मैंने ही

अब मेज़ पर दो प्यालियाँ ठंडी पड़ीं
तेरा हिस्सा भी गिराया मैंने ही

घर की सब दीवारें बची थीं शायद
नींव में ये पानी बहाया मैंने ही

## English Translation

I was the one who burned the letter in the stove;
I was the one who invited the smoke into the room.

Your key was now clenched in my fist;
I was the one who made this locked door.

That night, you told me only half of it;
I was the one who supplied the rest of the meaning.

I recounted every one of your mistakes to everyone;
I was the one who concealed my own share.

Now two cups sit cold upon the table;
I was the one who poured away your share.

Perhaps all the walls of the house could have survived;
I was the one who let this water run into the foundation.
