## मूल कविता (हिंदी/उर्दू)

रात कटोरे में आटा था
मेरी नींद संग भी फूल गया

कल रोटी कम पड़ने का डर
सुबह होते वह धूल गया

## English Translation

At night, there was dough in the bowl;
it rose along with my sleep.

Yesterday, I feared there would not be enough bread;
by morning, that fear had turned to dust.
