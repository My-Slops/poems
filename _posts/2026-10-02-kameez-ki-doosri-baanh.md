## मूल कविता (हिंदी/उर्दू)

रात भर कमीज़ भीगी थी
रस्सी ने उसे थामे रखा
सुबह हवा का हाथ लगा
एक बाँह पहले सूख गई
दूजी में रात बची थी
मैंने दोनों पहन लीं
घर से निकलते वक़्त लगा
आधा वादा निभ गया

## English Translation

The shirt stayed wet through the night; the clothesline held it fast.

Morning air laid a hand on it, and one sleeve dried first.

Night still remained in the other. I wore them both.

As I stepped out of the house, it felt as though half a promise had been kept.
