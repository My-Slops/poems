## मूल कविता (हिंदी/उर्दू)

बंद बही में सवाल रखा था
अपने हिस्से मलाल रखा था

तेरा ख़त जला दिया मगर
राख में तेरा ख़याल रखा था

मेरा दर खुला रहा रात भर
अपने अंदर ही जाल रखा था

तेरी तस्वीर थी मेज़ पे सीधी
पीछे दीवार का हाल रखा था

सुबह आई तो घर खाली मिला
मैंने रातों को सँभाल रखा था

## English Translation

In the closed ledger, I had kept a question;
for my own share, I had kept regret.

I burned your letter, and yet
I had kept the thought of you in the ash.

My door remained open all night;
it was inside myself that I had laid the trap.

Your photograph stood straight on the table;
behind it, I had preserved the wall's condition.

Morning came and found the house empty;
I was the one who had kept watch over the nights.
