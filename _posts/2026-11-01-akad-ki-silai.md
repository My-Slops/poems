## मूल कविता (हिंदी/उर्दू)

दर पीछे का खुला हुआ
नया कोट मेरी बाँह पर था
उसने दर्ज़ी नाम न पूछा
नौकर कहकर हाथ बढ़ाया
मैंने कोट वहीं रख डाला
मेहनत भी वहीं पड़ी थी
बस का आख़िरी सिक्का था
जूते घिसे, अकड़ बची

## English Translation

The back door stood open.
The new coat rested on my arm.
He did not ask the tailor's name;
he called me a servant and held out his hand.
I set the coat down right there;
my labor remained there too.
I still had the last coin for the bus;
my shoes wore down, my pride survived.
